Before You Start

Describe your business idea. AI agents map your real competitors from the live web and write your ideal business plan, before you spend a cent.

WHO IT'S FOR

  • First-time founders & small business owners (restaurants, shops, SaaS) about to launch
  • Today: days of googling, reviews & spreadsheets, stale in a month

WHAT IT DOES

  • 7 agents: Scout, Researcher, Analyst, Strategist, Planner, Watcher, Upgrader
  • Real prices, menus, reviews, locations, every fact sourced
  • Plan: pricing vs real competitors, best spot on the map, launch steps
  • Keeps watching & enriching · voice · MCP

WHY NOW

  • Agents read the live web cheaply: ~$0.17 & ~85 s per market
  • Data at the core: Aiven Postgres + pgvector; every page & fact stored, knowledge compounds across projects

EVIDENCE

  • 2,888 facts, 391 sources, 690 real prices, 100% sourced (plain Claude: 0%)
  • 1 enrich round: 89 → 302 facts, 0 → 79 priced items in 76 s
  • ~80 h of manual reading saved
FIRST USERS: incubators & accelerators (Station F, Bpifrance programs), business-plan advisors and franchise networks, founder communities. Free first report, paid plan for live watching & plan updates.
